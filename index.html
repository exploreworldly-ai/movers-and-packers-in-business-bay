How it works

Our Moving Process

A clear booking process helps you understand the work before the team arrives.

  1. 1

    Share both addresses

    Provide your pickup and delivery locations, including building names, unit numbers, and entrance instructions.

  2. 2

    Describe your inventory

    Tell us whether you're moving a studio, apartment, office, or villa — and flag fragile or oversized items.

  3. 3

    Review your quotation

    Check the quoted price, packing materials, labour, transport, dismantling, and any additional services.

  4. 4

    Confirm building access

    Arrange required approvals, service lifts, parking, and access instructions with building management.

  5. 5

    Moving day

    On the agreed date, the team carries out the booked packing, protection, loading, and transportation.

  6. 6

    Delivery and setup

    Belongings are unloaded at the destination, with assembly and placement completed per the agreed scope.
